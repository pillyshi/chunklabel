# Notes: gao-2023

> Gao, Y., Xiong, Y., Gao, X., et al. (2023). Retrieval-augmented generation for
> large language models: a survey. arXiv:2312.10997.

## Summary

A broad survey of RAG system architectures covering retrieval, augmentation, and
generation components. The chunking strategy discussion appears in the indexing
section and establishes why semantic chunking matters for retrieval quality.

## Chunking strategies covered

**Fixed-size chunking:** Split at a fixed token count (100, 256, 512).
Simple but ignores semantic boundaries; sentences may be truncated mid-thought.

**Recursive / sliding window:** Layered splits with overlap to preserve context
across boundaries. Reduces hard cuts but does not eliminate semantic fragmentation.

**Small2Big:** Retrieve on small units (sentences) but return a larger context
window (surrounding sentences) to the generator. Balances retrieval precision with
answer completeness.

**Semantic chunking (implied):** Split at semantic topic boundaries. The survey
notes this as the direction that improves retrieval precision without sacrificing
context.

## Key tradeoff

| Granularity | Retrieval precision | Context completeness |
|-------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Coarse (large chunks) | Lower | Higher |
| Fine (small chunks) | Higher | Lower |

"Selecting appropriate retrieval granularity during inference represents a simple
and effective strategy to improve the retrieval and downstream task performance of
dense retrievers."

## Key findings relevant to chunklabel

**Semantic chunking is the unsolved problem.** Fixed-size chunking is the dominant
practice despite known quality degradation. chunklabel directly addresses this:
LLM-assigned topic boundaries produce semantically motivated chunks without fixed
size assumptions.

**Chunk granularity affects downstream task quality.** The survey establishes that
the retrieval step is the primary bottleneck in RAG pipelines, and chunk quality
is the primary bottleneck in retrieval. This is the core motivation for the RAG
adapter idea (`ideas/rag-adapter-chunklabelsplitter.md`).

**Small2Big pattern.** chunklabel's `Chunk.start` / `Chunk.end` character offsets
enable exact implementation of Small2Big: retrieve on the `quote` (small) but
return a larger surrounding context (big) for the generator. This is a direct
downstream use case that the library should document.

## Limitations

- Survey does not benchmark chunking strategies directly; chunking quality is
  discussed qualitatively.
- Does not cover free-form category labels (chunklabel's core output).
- arXiv preprint; some claims may not be peer-reviewed.

## Actionable ideas

- Document the Small2Big usage pattern in chunklabel's README as a downstream
  RAG use case.
- Measure retrieval quality (e.g. recall@k) with chunklabel vs fixed-size chunking
  as part of the RAG adapter evaluation
